Our method

The training scale

Every lesson follows the classical training scale, one step at a time. Nothing is skipped, so horse and rider progress without rushing and without tension.

  1. i.Rhythm

    A steady, regular beat in walk, trot and canter.

  2. ii.Suppleness

    Loose muscles and a swinging back.

  3. iii.Contact

    A soft, steady connection to the rider’s hand.

  4. iv.Impulsion

    Energy that comes from the hindquarters.

  5. v.Straightness

    Hind feet follow the front feet.

  6. vi.Collection

    More weight behind, more lightness in front.

Faq

Questions we hear often

Do I need experience to start?

No. We teach every level, from complete beginners to intermediate and advanced riders, even if you have never ridden before. We start with an assessment ride to see where you are and plan your lessons from there.

What should I bring to my first lesson?

Comfortable riding breeches, riding boots, gloves and an approved riding helmet. If you don’t have any of these yet, don’t worry: we’ll lend them to you.

From what age do you take riders?

From age 8 in the young riders’ group. There is no upper age limit.

Are lessons private or in groups?

Adult lessons are private. Young riders ride in groups of four at most, and private lessons for children are also available if parents prefer.

Can I bring my own horse for a lesson?

Yes. You can box in for a single lesson or keep your horse here on livery. We’ll talk it through with you to find the best option.

Do you prepare riders for competitions?

Yes. We prepare the federation tests with you, come along to shows during the season and help you progress through your category.

What does livery include?

You rent a stable with feed included or, if you prefer, bring your own feed and hay. Daily paddock turnout can be added as an extra. Ask us for current prices.

Can you ride my horse when I can’t come?

Yes. If you are away, short on time or your horse needs extra work, our professional riders can ride and train it for you.

Do you run clinics?

Yes. Several times a year we invite professional riders to teach clinics, so you can take the next step in your dressage.
